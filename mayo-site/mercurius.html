Mercurius AI is a free tutor for iPhone.

It is an AI literacy tutor that opens with a question or a hint instead of the finished answer, then works toward the full explanation with you.

Free iPhone iOS 17 or later

trymercurius.com

What it does

  • Three modes

    Socratic, Discussion and Debate.

  • Lessons

    A learning path of 8 units and 35 lessons. The topics include how models work, bias, hallucinations and prompting.

  • Quizzes and unit checks

    A short quiz on what you just talked about, and a check at the end of each unit.

  • Photos

    Share an image, such as a diagram, a headline or a page of homework, and it talks through what is in it.

  • Privacy

    No account and no sign-up. The app uses a random ID on your phone and shows no ads. You can start over at any time with one tap.

What it will not do

It will not write your essays or do your homework, and it will not hand you an answer before you have done some thinking yourself.

An AI that teaches AI literacy

Mercurius AI runs on Claude, a large language model made by Anthropic.

So it is an AI that teaches students to be careful with AI, and that is on purpose. While you use it, you can watch how a model hedges, where it sounds more sure than it should, and what it assumes without saying so.

Who makes it

Shyam Christensen and Adam Keegan lead the app. Shyam is a co-founder and co-president of the club, and Adam is its vice president.

Mercurius AI came out on the App Store on June 15, 2026. It is the tutor described in “Teaching the Pause,” the poster the club’s board presented that month. The app used to be called Mercurius I.

Start an AI Literacy chapter at your school.

Mayo AI Literacy Club is a student-run club at Mayo High School in Rochester, Minnesota. We teach students how to use AI without quietly handing it their thinking. This fall we are opening the program to high schools across the country, and we need one student at each school willing to start a chapter.

Put your name in (Google Form, opens in a new tab) A Google Form. It takes about three minutes.

What a chapter needs

  • A founder

    One student, ideally a sophomore or junior.

  • An advisor

    One faculty advisor. The advisor attends meetings and signs off on room requests.

  • Meetings

    Once a month.

  • Events

    One AI literacy event each semester.

  • Cost

    Starting a chapter costs nothing.

What we provide

  • Lessons

    Curriculum and lesson materials the chapter can run as written, so nobody has to build a program from scratch.

  • Support

    Shyam Christensen and Adam Keegan will support each new chapter directly through its founding year. It happens over video, so it works the same in any state.

  • A vote

    A seat on our Central Board. Every chapter president holds a vote in how the network is run.

Who should start one

We are looking for a student who organizes things and finishes them. Usually that student is already running something else.

You don’t have to be a strong coder. A sophomore or junior gives the chapter a full year before it has to hand off to someone new.

Where we stand

In June 2026 the four of us on the board presented a poster, “Teaching the Pause: A Student-Led Model for Critical AI Literacy,” at the 43rd Annual Meeting of the American Association of Clinical Anatomists (AACA) in Rochester, June 12–15, 2026. The meeting had attendees from 23 countries.

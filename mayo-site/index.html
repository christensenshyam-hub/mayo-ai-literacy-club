What happens on Thursdays

One topic a week, talked through around a table. Some weeks are technical, some are about ethics, some are about using the tools well. These are the six topics we come back to.

  • AI ethicsWho is responsible when an autonomous system causes harm?
  • How AI worksHow does a neural network learn?
  • AI in healthWho answers for it when an AI used in patient care gets it wrong?
  • AI in financeWhat do AI fraud checks and investing tools mean for your own money?
  • PromptingHow do you write a prompt that gets you what you want?
  • Critical thinkingHow do you check AI-generated content before you repeat it?

Start a chapter at your school

Fall 2026

This fall we are opening the program to high schools across the country. We need one student at each school willing to start a chapter.

A chapter needs one student to found it and one faculty advisor. It meets monthly and runs one AI literacy event each semester. We provide lesson materials and support over video through the founding year. Starting a chapter costs nothing.

How chapters work

Mercurius AI

Free iPhone iOS 17 or later

Mercurius AI is the tutor described in “Teaching the Pause.” It opens with a question or a hint instead of the finished answer, then works toward the full explanation with you.

It has three modes: Socratic, Discussion and Debate. Its lessons cover how models work, bias, hallucinations and prompting. It will not write your essays or do your homework. It runs on Claude, a model made by Anthropic. Shyam Christensen and Adam Keegan lead the app.

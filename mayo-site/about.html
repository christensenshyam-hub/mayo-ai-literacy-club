Started in 2025 by two students at Mayo High School.

Shyam Christensen and Nathan Dozois noticed a gap. AI was already in our phones and our feeds, in hiring decisions and in healthcare. Almost no one our age had a clear picture of how it works or where it fails.

The story so far

2025
Shyam and Nathan started a student-run club at Mayo. It meets on Thursday mornings at 8:20 in the MHS Library Classroom.
March 2026
We started publishing members’ writing on this site, from a meeting recap to Nathan’s long read on Anthropic and the Pentagon. On March 19 we ran an advanced seminar, AI Dilemmas.
June 2026
The four of us on the board presented a poster, “Teaching the Pause: A Student-Led Model for Critical AI Literacy,” at the 43rd Annual Meeting of the American Association of Clinical Anatomists (AACA) in Rochester, June 12–15, 2026. On June 15, Mercurius AI, the tutor the poster describes, came out on the App Store.
Fall 2026
We held the fall interest meeting in the Mayo commons on Monday, September 28. We are also opening the program to high schools across the country.

What we think

We use AI, and we think students should learn to use it well. That starts with knowing how a model works and where it tends to fail.

We think working through a hard problem is part of learning it. A tool that hands you the finished answer skips that part. “Teaching the Pause” describes a tutor built to keep it in.

Three ways to use AI

The club sorts people who use AI into three groups. Our goal is to move every member toward the third.

The three groups of AI users, compared
Group How they use it Do they check it? Prompting
Group 1The Copy-Paster Takes the output as it comes, without questioning it. No. Minimal.
Group 2The AI User Uses it often, but only as a convenience. Sometimes. Basic.
Group 3The AI-Literate User Knows how it works and where it fails. Yes. Cross-checks what matters. Strategic.

How to join

Come to a meeting: Thursdays at 8:20 AM in the MHS Library Classroom.

Any Mayo High School student can come. You don’t need to have used AI before, and you don’t need to sign up for your first visit. If you would rather hear from us first, leave your name and we will email you. We post updates on Instagram at @mayoailiteracy.

At another high school? You can start a chapter there.

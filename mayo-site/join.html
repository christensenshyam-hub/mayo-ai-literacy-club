Three ways to take part

  1. 01

    Join a chapter

    Format

    Sign-up
    None for a first visit
    Experience
    None needed

    Who can come. Thursday meetings are for Mayo High School students. Outside visitors, including students from other schools, can’t attend school meetings.

  2. 02

    Start a chapter

    For a student founder and a faculty advisor at another high school

    What it takes, and what you get

    Needs
    One student founder and one faculty advisor
    Commits
    A meeting a month, and one AI literacy event each semester
    Cost
    Nothing
    Support
    Lessons you can run as written, video support through the founding year, and a vote on the Central Board

    Interest, not approval. Putting your name in is interest. A chapter exists only once a founder and an advisor are in place and the school’s own approval process has been followed.

Six topics we come back to on Thursdays.

Each one comes with a question of the kind we talk through at a meeting. Under the topics is our list of courses, videos, reading and tools.

Topics and questions

  • AI ethics

    Who is responsible when an autonomous system causes harm?

    Bias, fairness and privacy, and who answers for what an AI system decides.

    Read the AI Dilemmas seminar recap and Nathan Dozois’s “Who Gets to Tell the Robot No?”

  • How AI works

    How does a neural network learn?

    Machine learning, large language models and neural networks, explained in plain language.

    The 3Blue1Brown video “But what is a neural network?” walks through one, and TensorFlow Playground lets you watch one learn in your browser.

  • AI in health

    Who answers for it when an AI used in patient care gets it wrong?

    Where AI is used in diagnosis, drug discovery, mental health support and patient care.

    Princeton’s case studies include one on an automated healthcare app.

  • AI in finance

    What do AI fraud checks and investing tools mean for your own money?

    How AI is used in personal finance, investing and fraud detection.

  • Prompting

    How do you write a prompt that gets you what you want?

    Asking the right question, and steering what the tool gives back.

    Learn Prompting starts from the basics.

  • Critical thinking

    How do you check AI-generated content before you repeat it?

    Checking what an AI tells you, spotting misinformation, and knowing when to doubt an answer.

    Shyam Christensen’s essay “Vibe Coding Is Real” is about shipping AI-written code you don’t understand.

Resources

If a link stops working, tell us on Instagram at @mayoailiteracy.

  • Getting started

    • Free course

      Elements of AI

      An online introduction to AI from the University of Helsinki and MinnaLearn. It needs no programming or complicated math.

    • Interactive

      Machine Learning for Kids

      Train a computer to recognize text, numbers or pictures, then use what it learned in a Scratch project.

    • Video 19 min 3Blue1Brown

      But what is a neural network?

      An animated walk-through of what the neurons and layers in a neural network do, with handwritten digits as the example. It is chapter 1 of a series on deep learning.

  • Ethics and critical thinking

    • Case studies Princeton University

      Princeton Dialogues on AI and Ethics

      Eight fictional case studies written for discussion, on subjects such as hiring by machine, autonomous vehicles and an automated healthcare app. Each one is a PDF.

    • Organization

      Algorithmic Justice League

      Founded by Joy Buolamwini. It uses research and art to show how AI systems can be biased, including in facial recognition.

    • Toolkit Center for Humane Technology

      Youth Toolkit

      Seven guides on how social media is built to hold your attention and what you can do about it, with questions and activities for a class or a group.

    • Article Anthropic June 2024

      Claude’s Character

      How Anthropic chose and trained the traits of its Claude models, and why it wants people to know they are not talking to an objective source of truth.

  • Prompting

    • Free guide

      Learn Prompting

      A guide to writing prompts, from the basics to advanced techniques.

  • AI in the real world

    • Publication

      MIT Technology Review

      Its artificial intelligence section: reporting on AI research, products and policy.

    • Research institute

      AI Now Institute

      Research on what AI does to society, including work, health care and surveillance.

    • Data and charts

      Our World in Data

      Its artificial intelligence page: charts on AI progress, capabilities, risks and adoption.

  • Tools to try

    • AI assistant Anthropic

      Claude

      Anthropic’s assistant. Mercurius AI runs on it.

    • Hands-on tool Google

      Teachable Machine

      Train a model to recognize images, sounds or poses in your browser, without writing code.

    • Interactive demo

      TensorFlow Playground

      A small neural network in your browser. Change its layers and data, and watch it learn.

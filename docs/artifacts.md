# Artifacts catalogue (October 6, 2026)

Internal. Not published: Netlify publishes `mayo-site/` only.

This is the club's real evidence for the participation redesign: its photographs, signed writing, lesson materials and founding documents. Each entry says:

- what the artifact is and when it dates from;
- who made it;
- what it proves, with page or slide references (quotes are verbatim);
- whether it shows identifiable students;
- how the site could use it.

**Sources.** The founder's own files, which were read and not changed: the AACA poster on the Desktop, the PDFs and PNGs in `~/Downloads`, and the site's own `blog-content.json`, `events-data.json` and `assets/photos/`.

**Excluded:**

- the "ChatGPT Image …" files, which are AI-generated;
- `AI Literacy PBIS Video.MOV`;
- AP class materials, NBA photos and anything else unrelated to the club.

**Rendered images.** Rendered candidate images are in `.shots/artifacts/`, which is gitignored and so never deployed. They are listed in section 4. Nothing from this catalogue is on the site yet. A file goes into `mayo-site/assets/` only after its permission question in `docs/content-needs.md` (items 19–38) is answered.

**Dates.** A "file date" is the file's own creation or modification date. It is NOT a date stated in the document, and must never be published as one.

### A3. "AI Literacy Governance Constitution" (titled "MAYO AI LITERACY CONSTITUTION")
- **File:** `~/Downloads/AI Literacy Governance Constitution.pdf`. Five pages. File date: September 6, 2026. An older `Mayo_AI_Literacy_Constitution.pdf` (file date August 6, 2026) also exists. It was not in scope and was not reviewed.
- **Date:** None in the document. The "ADOPTED BY" block on p. 5 has four BLANK signature lines: President, Vice President, Chapter President of the founding Chapter, and Faculty Advisor of the founding Chapter. So this copy is unadopted.
- **Authors:** Not stated. It names "Shyam Christensen, founder of the Organization" as first President (Art. III §1, p. 2).
- **What it proves:**
  - It proves how the network is designed to govern itself. The full extracted facts, with page references, are in `participation-redesign.md` § "Additional verified facts (artifacts)".
  - The essentials:
    - two levels, Chapters and a Central Board (Art. II §1, p. 1);
    - Chapter Presidents "sit on the Central Board as voting members; the Chapters are governed with the Board rather than by it" (Art. II §3, p. 2);
    - "Each Chapter President holds one (1) vote on the Central Board" (Art. III §3, p. 2);
    - the Board "does not direct the internal programming of any Chapter" (Art. II §3, p. 2);
    - "the President votes only to break a tie" (Art. IV §3, p. 3).
  - Its preamble is the clearest statement of purpose the club has: "to teach our peers how these systems work, where they fail, and when not to use them … and to bring organized student judgment to the adults writing the rules" (p. 1).
- **Identifiable students:** Names Shyam Christensen only. No images.
- **Site use:**
  - The source for one or two plain sentences on `/chapters` about what a Central Board vote means, once the founder confirms the document is in force (#29).
  - Possibly a chapter resource itself, as a downloadable founding document.
- **Cautions, which must be resolved before anything new from it goes on the site:**
  1. It is unsigned (p. 5).
  2. Its scope is "a network of school-based Chapters within Rochester Public Schools" (Art. I §1, p. 1), while the brief and the site open chapters "to high schools across the country".
  3. "Chapters shall meet regularly during the school year, ordinarily weekly" (Art. V §1, p. 3), while the brief says "The chapter meets monthly".
  4. A Chapter is established "by resolution of the Central Board upon identifying a faculty advisor and at least five (5) prospective members" (Art. II §2, pp. 1–2). The brief doesn't mention five members or a Board resolution.
  5. Art. VI §4 (p. 4): "The Organization does not act as a representative, reseller, or promoter of any commercial product or vendor, including products created by its own members." The site promotes Mercurius AI, built by members (#32).
  6. The name is "Mayo AI Literacy" (Art. I §1), not "Mayo AI Literacy Club". The spec says to keep the club name on the site.
  7. Drafting slips:
     - Art. V §3 (pp. 3–4) refers to "Article VII, Section 3" for removing the President, but VII §3 is Dissolution;
     - "the Founding Presidents term" (Art. III §1) is never defined.
